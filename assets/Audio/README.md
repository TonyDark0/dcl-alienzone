# Runtime audio

The audio director expects these music files:

```text
assets/Audio/music/az_mus_lobby_containment_loop_v001.mp3
assets/Audio/music/az_mus_match_alienzone_loop_v001.mp3
```

- `lobby_containment`: calm tension for lobby, results and rematch.
- `match_alienzone`: active gameplay and takeover music.

Use seamless stereo MP3 at 44.1 kHz and 128–160 kbps. Future SFX belong in `sfx/` as OGG and ambience in `amb/` as MP3. Follow `az_<amb|mus|sfx>_<description>_v###.<ext>`.

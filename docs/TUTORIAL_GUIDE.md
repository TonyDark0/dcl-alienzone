# Illustrated Objective guide

Entry: **HOW TO PLAY → OBJECTIVE**. Three pages, two whole illustrated steps per page. Previous/Next controls stay outside the content. No stock scrollbar, dragging or hover is required. The story and objective remain visible on every page.

**Lore:** A baby alien hatched inside the ship. When its timer expires, it attacks the astronaut carrying it.

**Goal:** Avoid the attacks and finish all three rounds with the most points.

## 1. Move and throw

![Move](../assets/Images/ui/tutorial/v001/az_ui_tutorial_move_v001.png)

**MOVE & EVADE — WASD / mobile joystick.** Run to evade carriers, approach eligible targets and collect coins. Everyone has the same base speed; card effects can change it.

![Throw](../assets/Images/ui/tutorial/v001/az_ui_tutorial_throw_v001.png)

**GET CLOSE & THROW — Space / tap THROW ALIEN.** The button enables only when carrying an arrived alien near an eligible target. Base range is 1.5m, with the existing bounded server-observed latency window. A successful transfer gives +1. Mere overlap never passes it; held Space never auto-repeats. The immediate giver is return-protected for 2.5s. E no longer throws. All ownership, immunity and turn checks remain server-side.

## 2. Cards and coins

![Cards](../assets/Images/ui/tutorial/v001/az_ui_tutorial_deck_v001.png)

**PREPARE & PLAY CARDS — F / tap the ready card.** Prepare three ordered cards per round, nine total. Only the queue head activates. Valid activations give +1; Epics have the existing conditional second point. Reordering never pauses the chase. Unused cards expire at round end. General repeats across rounds; each Epic type occupies one slot per match; Rare powers are board pickups, not deck cards.

![Coins](../assets/Images/ui/tutorial/v001/az_ui_tutorial_coins_v001.png)

**COLLECT SHARED COINS — first contact gives +1.** Ten coins spawn per round, thirty per match. First contact collects a coin once for everyone; no mid-round replacement. Leftovers expire at takeover. These are match points, not currency.

## 3. Survival and containment

![Danger](../assets/Images/ui/tutorial/v001/az_ui_tutorial_danger_v001.png)

**WATCH THE ALIEN — shared timer.** Only the original alien attacks its holder at expiry. Holding gives no points. Finish a round without holding either alien for +1. Freeze does not pause the timer.

![Contain](../assets/Images/ui/tutorial/v001/az_ui_tutorial_contain_v001.png)

**CONTAIN THE ATTACK — click / tap the lit targets.** Only the victim performs the five timed inputs. At least three hits give one point. The crew contains the first two attacks regardless of performance. The third attack ends the match even if the victim earns the final bonus; nobody is eliminated early.

## Assets and implementation

Six original 256×256 RGBA PNG pictograms, generated by `scripts/Generate-TutorialIcons.ps1` from vector drawing primitives. Labels remain live English UI text; no baked keyboard text, AI image dependency, remote URL or new runtime package. Shared palette: navy, cyan, lime, violet and warm coin gold. Decorative images do not intercept input. Keep the fixed 1920×1080 renderer and its SDK mobile override; compensate narrow layouts locally, not through a second global scale.

Content: `src/ui/state/tutorial.ts`. Presentation: `src/ui/components/ObjectiveInstructions.tsx`. Placement/pagination: `src/ui/screens/LobbyScreen.tsx`. Keyboard mapping: `src/client/chaseControls.ts`. Mobile keeps the existing explicit THROW button and native movement joystick.

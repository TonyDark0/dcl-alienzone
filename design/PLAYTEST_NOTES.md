# Featured-game research notes

Source: the creator's own play observations shared during this project's development. These are subjective design observations, not independent audits of other developers' code, completion status or assets. Titles use the creator's spelling. Exact dates, device/client versions and any missing durations must be supplied by the creator.

| Game reported by owner    | Owner rating | Observations                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Lesson for Alienzone                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------- | ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Shroom Zom                | 8/10         | Attractive forest/puzzle exploration; eight sounding aquatic platforms and a music hint. Note triggering/reset behavior was unclear; no clear next-day reason was found during that visit.                                                         | Make action feedback and reset rules explicit. A clever mechanic alone does not establish later return.                                                                                             |
| Old McTiger's Farm        | 2/10         | About ten minutes exploring; unclear onboarding and use of points. Inventory/shop appeared unavailable during the visit; rare animal catches required one click. Visual styles felt inconsistent to the owner.                                     | Make progress usable and its purpose visible. Use a consistent asset/atlas language and avoid empty navigation options.                                                                             |
| Antrom RPG / Dice Masters | 7/10         | Skippable voiced lore introduction, players present, daily chest/quests/energy and crafting. UI text/images overlapped in places; dense menus increased effort. Equipment not changing the visible avatar reduced the owner's sense of atmosphere. | Keep a concise optional introduction, clear return motivation and readable menus. Astronaut proxies support art coherence; this is a project preference, not a claim that native avatars are wrong. |

## V0 proposal

Polish and validate the existing mobile-first social game: complete a three-round match, use cards during other players' turns, see a clear takeover, compare persistent records and rematch. Keep the approved four-parcel layout, eight seats and Decentraland-native backend. Scope and delivery timing are proposals until agreed with Creator Success.

Known incomplete production work: final alien/egg/VFX exports, dedicated match music, fresh device performance measurements and production multiplayer/persistence tests. See [scope](../docs/SCOPE.md), [asset contract](../docs/GLB_NAMING_AND_ASSET_PATHS.md) and [GDD risk](gdd.md#9-4-week-plan-v1-scope).

## Funding is not triggered by GitHub publication

The current guide lists **$1,000 for V0**, paid after the **agreed V0 scope is completed and playtestable**. Creating a repository, applying or receiving an acceptance does not by itself establish that the payment milestone has been met. Contract, payment method, invoice/KYC requirements and actual payment date must be confirmed directly with the program; this project does not invent those terms.

The guide currently lists an initial application window through **November 30, 2026**, with weekly review. This is time-sensitive information, not a guaranteed review or acceptance date.
